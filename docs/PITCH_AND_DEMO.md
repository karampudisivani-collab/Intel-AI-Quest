# Pitch and Judge Demo

## 60-second pitch
Gen Z already uses AI, but many shoppers still see a laptop as a processor, a price tag and a colour. That is the adoption gap.

Intel AI Quest turns the retail store into a personal discovery experience. In two to three minutes, shoppers choose who they are — Student, Creator, Hustler or Gamer — and play quick real-life challenges. They explore practical AI workflows, learn the roles of CPU, GPU and NPU in plain language, and receive a recommendation based on their needs rather than appearance alone.

The experience ends with a clear next step: try a supported AI feature on a real Intel Core Ultra laptop with a store expert.

For retailers, the platform can also provide anonymous aggregate engagement insights: which personas shoppers choose, how many finish and which demos generate interest.

We are not asking Gen Z to believe another AI advertisement. We are giving them a reason to experience the difference.

## 2–3 minute live demo
### 0:00–0:15 — Hook
Say: “You use AI every day. But does your PC help you get more from it? Let's find out.”
Click Start Quest.

### 0:15–0:30 — Persona
Choose Student (or the persona most relatable to the audience).
Say: “The experience adapts to the way a shopper actually uses a laptop.”

### 0:30–1:20 — Challenges
Answer the three scenarios. Explain that they are everyday decisions, not a technical exam.
Say: “We teach the benefit through an action, not a specification sheet.”

### 1:20–1:45 — Result
Show persona, score and configuration checklist.
Say: “We recommend what to compare — memory, GPU, display, battery and software support — not a random model or unsupported live price.”

### 1:45–2:15 — AI Studio
Paste a short set of notes and generate a summary.
Say: “This prototype can call a cloud AI service. It is clearly labelled. The on-device proof point is a separate live demonstration on the Intel laptop.”

### 2:15–2:40 — AI PC explainer and close
Show CPU, GPU and NPU.
Say: “The CPU handles general computing, the GPU excels at parallel graphics workloads, and the NPU efficiently accelerates supported AI workloads. Now let's try a compatible AI feature on the actual laptop.”

## Judge Q&A

### What problem are you solving?
Shoppers may use AI daily but do not understand why AI PC hardware matters. Retail conversations often overemphasize price and aesthetics. We make relevant use cases tangible before purchase.

### What is actually AI-powered?
The optional AI Studio can use Gemini through a cloud API. The offline fallback is deterministic formatting, not AI. The quest scoring and recommendations are rules-based. The on-device AI proof point requires a supported application running on an Intel AI PC.

### How do you prove Intel's advantage?
Demonstrate the exact Intel Core Ultra device and compatible application. Compare like-for-like laptop configurations, software support and measured results. Do not claim blanket superiority over every competitor.

### Why use an NPU?
An NPU is a dedicated engine designed to efficiently accelerate supported neural-network workloads. CPU, GPU and NPU have complementary roles. Software decides which engine is used for a given feature.

### How do you measure business impact?
Track anonymous starts, completions, AI Studio use, recommendation saves and accepted staff demos. Run a store pilot and compare engagement before claiming conversion improvement.

### How do you protect privacy?
The prototype does not ask for personal identity details. Session analytics use anonymous IDs and store persona, score, budget band and timestamp. Avoid sensitive uploads. Cloud AI input is sent to the configured provider; users are warned not to paste sensitive information.

### What happens if internet fails?
The game and frontend work locally. AI Studio uses a deterministic fallback. SQLite analytics require the local backend. A backup demo recording is also prepared.

## Evidence to collect before final submission
- Actual device make/model and processor SKU.
- NPU capability from official product documentation.
- Application name/version and supported feature.
- Whether feature runs locally, in cloud, or hybrid.
- Screen recording of real device demonstration.
- Dated source links for any specifications or comparisons.
- Pilot results only if actually measured.
